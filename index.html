Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

Made for how people in Malaysia actually pay: cash, e-wallets, bank transfers and cards.

Add spending in seconds

Start with the amount, then merchant, category, date and a payment tag such as Cash, TNG or Bank. Add notes or item lines if you want. “Save & add another” keeps you moving. Income works too.

Snap a receipt or screenshot

Take a photo of a paper receipt or choose a payment screenshot. The text is read on your iPhone using Apple’s Vision framework. You check the merchant, amount and date, then save. Nothing is saved automatically.

Works with Shortcuts

Set up a Back Tap shortcut that takes a screenshot and opens it in DuitSnap for review. On iOS 17 or later, a Wallet transaction automation can pass the merchant and amount from an Apple Pay tap. Several screenshots queue and open one by one.

Budgets and clear insights

Set a monthly budget and per-category limits, with optional alerts when you get close. See spending by category, daily spending, your daily average and top merchants. Recurring bills and income can be recorded for you.

Also included

  • 16 built-in categories, plus your own custom categories.
  • Category suggestions for hundreds of common Malaysian merchants. Your own “remember merchant category” choice always comes first.
  • Search and filter your records by date, category, payment tag and more.
  • Export CSV, or save a JSON backup and restore it later, through the iOS share sheet. You choose where the file goes.
  • Optional daily reminder (9:00 PM by default, or turn it off). It is skipped on days you have already logged something.
  • Optional receipt-photo keeping: off by default, or keep for 3, 6 or 12 months or indefinitely, inside the app only.
  • Light and dark mode. English, Bahasa Melayu and Simplified Chinese.

Your money records stay on your phone.

DuitSnap has no account, no server and no cloud. We never receive your spending data.

  • Records, budgets, categories and settings are stored only in the app on your iPhone.
  • Receipt text is read on your device. Images are not uploaded.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no bank login.
  • The app only uses an image when you take, choose or share one. It does not scan your photo library in the background.
  • Backups go only where you send them.
